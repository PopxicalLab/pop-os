General Management Core Skills

2 skills
Project Management
The meta-skill of getting things done by orchestrating people, time, and resources. It synthesizes hard skills (budgeting, scheduling), soft skills (leadership, empathy), and strategic skills (scope management, critical thinking).
Conflict Management
The ability to resolve disputes, negotiate competing priorities, and maintain morale. Functions both as a critical tool within project management and as a highly transferable standalone life skill.

Creative Studio Project Manager Skills

10 skills
Creative Translation
Translating vague, business-oriented client briefs into concrete, actionable tasks for creative teams.
Subjective Feedback Management
Parsing emotional or subjective client feedback (e.g., "I don't like the vibe") to uncover the underlying reasons, providing clear direction for revisions.
Workflow Empathy
Understanding the realities of the creative process, including accurate time estimations for subjective work and recognising when creatives need uninterrupted focus.
Resource Allocation for Creatives
Matching specific creative personalities, strengths, and design styles to the appropriate projects and clients.
Scope & Budget Management
Protecting the project's profitability by preventing over-servicing and endless creative refinement loops.
Project Scheduling & Phasing
Building realistic timelines (e.g., Gantt charts) that factor in internal reviews, client feedback loops, and inevitable revision cycles.
Risk Mitigation
Anticipating potential project roadblocks, dependencies, and bottlenecks before they happen and developing strategies to minimise their impact.
Tool Proficiency
Mastery of project management software (Asana, Monday, Jira) and familiarity with creative file ecosystems (Figma, Adobe CC).
Shielding the Team
Acting as a gatekeeper to intercept chaotic client requests, filtering them into organised, calm updates for the team.
Cheerleading & Morale Building
Celebrating team wins, enforcing breaks to prevent creative burnout, and maintaining positive energy during tedious revision phases.

Creative Technologist Skills

9 skills
Immersive & Spatial Computing
Familiarity with AR, VR, MR, and XR technologies, often involving game engines like Unreal Engine or Unity.
Creative Coding & Real-Time Graphics
Programming interactive visuals using tools such as TouchDesigner, Max/MSP, Processing, or WebGL/Three.js.
Artificial Intelligence (AI) Fluency
Leveraging generative AI workflows (LLMs, image generation) for brainstorming, asset creation, or building responsive user experiences.
Physical Computing
Building interactive hardware installations using sensors, microcontrollers (Arduino, Raspberry Pi), and projection mapping.
Web & App Development
Foundational knowledge of front-end development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React) or mobile app prototyping.
Rapid Prototyping
The mindset of quickly sketching, hacking, and turning abstract ideas into testable experiments rather than seeking immediate perfection.
Design Fluency & Storytelling
Understanding UI/UX principles, utilising tools like Figma or Adobe CC, and using technology to communicate narratives and solve human needs.
Translation & Communication
Bridging the gap between disciplines by explaining technical constraints to creatives and abstract design goals to engineers.
Innovation Management
Balancing wild, ambitious creative visions with actual technical feasibility, budgets, and hardware limitations.

Digital Content Creation & Animation Skills

10 skills
3D Modeling
Creating three-dimensional digital representations of objects, environments, and characters using specialised software like Maya or Blender.
Rigging
Building the digital skeleton and control mechanisms for 3D models to allow for realistic movement and animation.
Animation
Bringing static 2D or 3D objects, characters, and environments to life through movement, sequencing, and timing.
VFX (Visual Effects)
Creating and integrating artificial imagery or effects (like explosions, magic, or environmental simulations) into live-action footage or digital scenes.
Motion Design
Applying graphic design principles to filmmaking and video production through the use of animation, typography, and visual effects.
Concept Art
Visualising and illustrating initial ideas, characters, environments, or moods to guide the overall aesthetic and visual direction of a project.
Compositing
Combining visual elements from separate sources (like 3D renders, live-action, and matte paintings) into a single, seamless image or final frame.
Lighting
Simulating realistic or stylised illumination in digital environments to enhance mood, depth, storytelling, and composition.
Texturing
Applying digital surface qualities, colours, and material properties to 3D models to make them look realistic or fit a specific style.
Rendering
The automated computational process of generating final 2D images or animation sequences from complex 3D models, textures, and lighting setups.

Marketing & Growth Skills

8 skills
Brand Strategy
Defining the core identity, voice, and positioning of a brand in the market to ensure consistent messaging across all touchpoints.
Content Marketing
Creating and distributing valuable, relevant content (blogs, videos, newsletters) to attract, engage, and retain a clearly defined audience.
SEO & SEM
Optimising digital content to rank higher in organic search engine results (SEO) and managing paid search advertising campaigns (SEM).
Social Media Strategy
Developing and executing tailored campaigns across social platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok) to build community and drive engagement.
Data Analytics & Reporting
Tracking key performance indicators (KPIs) and using analytics tools to measure campaign success, gather insights, and optimise future strategies.
Campaign Management
Overseeing the end-to-end execution of multi-channel marketing campaigns, ensuring alignment with overall business goals, budgets, and timelines.
Performance Marketing
Driving measurable, conversion-focused results through targeted paid advertising (such as pay-per-click, programmatic ads, and social ads).
Email Marketing
Designing, automating, and managing email campaigns to nurture leads, communicate with audiences, retain customers, and drive sales.
