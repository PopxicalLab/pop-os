October 2026

2 changes
Jobs
Amounts with cents now show exactly as entered. A cost of RM 44.70 shows as RM 44.70 instead of being rounded to RM 45, and the same goes for totals, net, invoices and the Jobs list. Whole amounts still show without decimals (RM 5,000).
Jobs
You can now attach files to a job: contracts, purchase orders, signed quotations, invoices, receipts, briefs and so on. Open a job, scroll to Files, and drop files in (or click to choose). Each file can be tagged with a type and a short note. Click a PDF or image to view it, or use Download. Files sit on the Pop OS server, up to 25 MB each, and like the rest of the job only the money roles can see them — production-only staff can't.

September 2026

33 changes
Jobs
You can overrule the suggested PPM quadrant and say why. If the quadrant you pick on a job differs from the suggestion (say, a small job for an MNC client kept as a Strategic bet because more work is likely), the job page shows an Overridden box with a space for the reason. On Sales cards and in the List view, overridden quadrants carry a ✎ mark — hover it to read the reason.
Sales
The status dropdown is back on each Pipeline card. Pick a new stage from the list, or keep dragging cards between columns — both work.
Sales
Sales List view is easier to read: open dropdowns now use the app's colours instead of pale text on white, and the list uses the full width of the screen — the Add-a-lead form folds away while you're in List view (use + Add lead to bring it back). Column headers are shorter and long names are cut to one line (hover to see the full text), so all the columns fit on a normal screen.
Sales
New List view on the Sales tab, next to Leads over time and Pipeline — one row per lead, laid out like the old Airtable "All opportunities" table: status, priority, account, primary contact, sales PIC, completion, estimated value, invoiced %, paid % and payment date, plus Pop OS extras (company, PPM quadrant, project status, won / created dates). Most cells can be edited right in the list, click a column header to sort, and the total value of what's listed shows at the bottom. Use ⚙ Columns to choose which columns you see — your choice is remembered for your login on that computer. Click a lead's name to open its job.
Projects
Money is now only visible on jobs. The project page is production-only: dates, team, priority, quadrant, skills, capacity and timeline. The PPM box, the Est. Value / Duration / Complexity / Client tier / Margin list columns and the money fields on the "Add a project" form are gone — value, PPM and the Drain approvals are set on the project's job (Jobs tab). Admin, Producer, PM, Finance and Sales see a Money & PPM → Open job link on each project. Team Leads and Staff no longer receive any deal values, costs or invoice amounts anywhere in Pop OS (including the projects CSV export and My Work), and the system now refuses money requests from their logins outright, rather than just hiding the tabs.
Jobs
PPM now happens on the job — before quoting. Each job page has a PPM assessment section: complexity, duration, quadrant, a live recommendation (plus "similar past jobs"), and the Drain gate approvals. Leads at Proposal or Negotiation show an Assess (PPM) → link on their Sales card, and assessed cards show their quadrant (and "needs approval" for an unapproved Drain). A Drain job can no longer be turned into a project until both Exec and Producer have approved it. When a won lead becomes a project, it now starts with the quadrant from the assessment and the recommended priority instead of always Gold / P2. The project page keeps priority editable and shows the rest of PPM read-only, with an "Assess on the job →" link.
Jobs
New Jobs tab in the top menu — every won piece of work in one list, with its sales stage, production status, deadline, producer / PM, value, costs, net, invoiced / paid % and any unpaid or overdue invoices side by side. Search, and switch between In progress / Delivered / All. Click a row to open the job, which now shows three sections: Sales (who won it, when, client, contact), Production (project status, dates, team, who's booked this week) and Money. Jobs show money, so the tab is for Admin, Producer, PM, Finance and Sales only.
Sales
New Job page — every deal and its project now sit under one record. Click Open job → on any Sales pipeline card to see everything money-related in one place: value, margin target, client tier, invoiced / paid %, costs, and all Autocount quotations and invoices (push new ones from here too), plus a Value / Costs / Net / Net margin summary. The project page now shows production only — dates, team, skills, capacity — with an Open job → link for the money. Projects that were created without a sales lead got a job automatically; those jobs don't count toward commission or sales targets.
Capacity
New Show in capacity reports switch on each person (People → Edit, admin only). Untick it for bosses and they'll no longer appear in the weekly Mattermost capacity post, the Dashboard's "this week" panel, the "who's free" lists or staffing suggestions — so they stop showing up as "100% free". You can still book them on the Capacity board, and when you do, they still show up under that project in the weekly post.
Production
Removed the Assets and Change Requests tabs — deliverables, creative sign-off and client change requests are now managed in our project-management tool instead. Along with them: the sign-off queue and "Assigned to me" list on My Work, the ✔ sign-off toggle on People, the asset count and badges on staff profiles, and the asset summary on Production lane cards. A project can now be marked Delivered at any time. Existing asset and change-request records have been permanently deleted.
Sales
Removed the status dropdown from each lead card on the Sales pipeline to keep cards cleaner. To move a lead to a different stage, drag its card into the new column.
Projects
Added a Detailed timeline link on the project detail page — paste in wherever the PM keeps the real day-by-day schedule (a Google Calendar, a Goodday project, a Gantt board, anything the team can open directly). An Open ↗ link appears once it's filled in. Start date / Deadline are unchanged and still drive PPM and the Gantt view.
Login
Added a "Stay logged in" checkbox on the login page, ticked by default. With it on, you stay signed in for 30 days instead of the usual 12 hours — untick it on a shared computer if you'd rather not.
Capacity
Fixed a bug where the "weekend" badge stayed on an allocation after you brought it back under 100% — it now clears automatically once weekend work is no longer needed.
Sales
The WhatsApp group messages for new leads and lead stage changes have been switched off — Mattermost now does that job. Nothing changes on your side, as long as the right Mattermost channel has a rule set up (Admin → Mattermost Notifications).
Sales
Mattermost can now announce new leads and lead stage changes the moment they happen — set up under Admin → Mattermost Notifications. You choose which stages to be told about (for example only Won and Lost), whether the estimated value is shown, and whether the person who closed the deal is @mentioned. These replace the old WhatsApp group messages.
Admin
Added Mattermost Notifications. Pop OS can now post to Mattermost channels or send private messages to individual people. In the Admin tab you choose which message to send, who receives it (a channel, one person, or several), and when — days and times are in Malaysia time (GMT+8). The first message available is the weekly capacity board, and each rule lets you choose what goes in it: a per-person table (with over-allocated and free flags), a per-project view, and a list of people with spare capacity — for this week or next, limited to a company or specific departments. Each rule has Send test and Run now buttons so you can check it works without waiting for the scheduled time.
Projects
Every project must now have a company set — LPS, PXL, or Group — it can no longer be left blank. Fixed a bug along the way where the company picked on the Add Project form was silently ignored and the project saved with no company at all.
Projects
Added a Group company option for projects — for work that belongs to the studio as a whole rather than one side. A Group project always shows up no matter which company (LPS/PXL) the header filter is set to, and gets its own grey badge to tell it apart.
Sales
Added a Leads over time view, alongside Pipeline, in the Sales tab (it opens by default) — a stacked bar per month or quarter showing how many leads landed in each pipeline stage, with a table view for anyone who'd rather read the numbers. Quarter view always shows Q1–Q4 of the current year, even quarters with no leads yet.
Sales
Producers can now access the Sales pipeline tab, so they can view and manage leads alongside the Sales team.
Sales
Lead titles are now editable directly on the card — click the title, type, click away to save. Long titles clamp to 2 lines (hover to see the full title) so cards stay a consistent height, and show in full while editing.
Login
Added password reset. Forgotten your password? Click Forgot password? on the login page and a reset link will be emailed to you. Admins can also generate and hand out a reset password directly from the User Manager.
Guide
The user guide now covers every module in the app — Projects, Capacity, Assets, Production Lanes, Change Requests, Clients, Sales Performance & Commissions, People & Skills, Committees, Staffing Board, and Admin & Audit Log — and a few access-related descriptions were corrected along the way.
Capacity
Added a studio-wide average utilization strip above the board, showing Studio / LPS / PXL averages for the selected week at a glance.
Projects
Removed the Timeline (Gantt) and Calendar views — List and Kanban cover it better. List's Deadline column is now sortable and shows days left / overdue on each row.
Capacity
Fixed the editable allocation % input's "%" sign not matching the number's color.
Capacity
Added a By person / By project toggle to the Capacity board, with collapsible groups (+ a "Collapse all" button) for boards with many people. Added an Unallocated this week list at the bottom, sorted by company, so it's clear at a glance who's free. Allocation % is now editable inline on the board.
Dashboard
Added a Sales Pipeline summary to the top of the Dashboard — deal counts and value per stage, plus a "Needs attention" list of high-priority open deals.
Sales
On a won lead's card, the project name is now a clickable link straight to that project's page.
Projects
Fixed adding a project cost from the project detail page silently failing and reloading the page instead of saving.
Sales
Added a Completed kanban stage to the Sales pipeline; fixed commission figures not syncing when a project's cost lines change.
Projects
Fixed start date not saving — it was silently failing when sent to the database. Save errors on the project detail page now show up on screen instead of failing silently.
